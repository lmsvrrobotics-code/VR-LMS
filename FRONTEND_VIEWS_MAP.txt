STUDENT LOGIN
  ↓
Student Dashboard Loads

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
Click "Enter Full Screen" or Lesson
  ↓
Navigate to /courses/play/{courseId}/{lessonId}
  ↓
Course Player Loads
  ↓
Watch Video, Mark Complete, Go to Next Lesson


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                      ACCESS CONTROL AT EACH LEVEL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

My Courses Tab
├─ Check: Is student in any batch?
├─ Yes: Show courses from batches
└─ No: Show "No batches" message

Course Details Page
├─ Check: Is course in student's batch(es)?
├─ Yes: Show course details ✨
└─ No: Show 403 Access Denied error

Course Player
├─ Check: Same as above + lesson unlocked?
├─ Yes: Play lesson
└─ No: Show locked message
